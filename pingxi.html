🏮 Pingxi Lantern Festival · Taiwan

4 presentadores · unos 5 minutos cada uno, todo en inglés. Lo que está entre corchetes es una acción (mostrar un objeto). La traducción es solo para estudiar.

Student 1 · Origin

Hello everyone! Welcome to our stand. My name is ___. Today we are going to talk about the Pingxi Lantern Festival. It is a beautiful festival from Taiwan. [Point to Taiwan on the map]

Taiwan is an island in Asia, near China. Pingxi is a small town in the mountains of Taiwan. It is famous for its sky lanterns. A sky lantern is a paper lantern with a small fire inside. The hot air makes it fly up into the sky, like a balloon.

Now I want to tell you the story. Long ago, the people of Pingxi had a big problem. Bad men came to the mountain villages to steal. When the bandits came, the families ran and hid in the mountains. The men stayed to protect the village. After the danger, the men sent up a sky lantern. It was a signal for the families. It said: "We are safe. You can come home." [Show the picture]

Since then, sky lanterns are a symbol of peace and hope. People also say that the lanterns carry messages to the sky.

Today, the lanterns are not only signals. People use them to send their wishes. The festival is part of the Lantern Festival, the last day of the Lunar New Year celebration.

Now I will pass to my partner. Thank you!

Student 2 · How and why we celebrate

Hello! My name is ___. I am going to tell you how people celebrate the Pingxi Lantern Festival.

First, each person chooses a lantern. The lanterns have different colors. Many people say that every color has a meaning. For example, red is for good health and yellow is for money. [Show the colored lanterns]

Then, people write their wishes on the lantern with a black marker. Some wishes are: "I want to pass my exam", "I want my family to be healthy", or "I want to be happy". A big lantern has four sides, so a family can write many wishes together. [Show a lantern with a wish]

Next, they light the lantern. They hold it with friends or family. They count: "three, two, one!" And they let it go. The lantern flies up into the dark sky. Hundreds of lanterns fly at the same time. It looks like a river of lights. People take photos and make a silent wish.

In other parts of Taiwan, there are big parades with giant lanterns in animal shapes. People also eat sweet rice balls called tangyuan. The round shape means that the family is together. They also play riddle games. The riddles are written on lanterns.

Why do people celebrate? They celebrate to start the new year with hope. They send their wishes to the sky, and they hope that they come true.

Thank you! Now my partner will tell you when it is and what you need.

Traducción al español

¡Hola! Mi nombre es ___. Les voy a contar cómo celebra la gente el Festival de los Faroles de Pingxi.

Primero, cada persona elige un farol. Los faroles tienen diferentes colores. Mucha gente dice que cada color tiene un significado. Por ejemplo, el rojo es para la buena salud y el amarillo es para el dinero. [Muestra los faroles de colores]

Luego, la gente escribe sus deseos en el farol con un marcador negro. Algunos deseos son: "Quiero pasar mi examen", "Quiero que mi familia esté sana" o "Quiero ser feliz". Un farol grande tiene cuatro lados, así que una familia puede escribir muchos deseos juntos. [Muestra un farol con un deseo]

Después, encienden el farol. Lo sostienen con amigos o familia. Cuentan: "¡tres, dos, uno!" Y lo sueltan. El farol sube al cielo oscuro. Cientos de faroles vuelan al mismo tiempo. Parece un río de luces. La gente toma fotos y hace un deseo en silencio.

En otras partes de Taiwán hay grandes desfiles con faroles gigantes con forma de animales. La gente también come bolitas de arroz dulces llamadas tangyuan. La forma redonda significa que la familia está unida. También juegan a las adivinanzas. Las adivinanzas están escritas en los faroles.

¿Por qué celebra la gente? Celebran para empezar el año nuevo con esperanza. Envían sus deseos al cielo y esperan que se cumplan.

Student 3 · When and what we need

Hello! My name is ___. I will tell you when the Lantern Festival is and what you need.

First, when. The Lantern Festival is every year. It is the fifteenth day of the Lunar New Year, in February or March. It is the first full moon of the year. In Pingxi, people also release lanterns on other days of the year, but the Lantern Festival is the most special day. [Point to the calendar]

Now, what do you need? Let me show you. [Show each item]

Number one: a paper lantern. You need colored paper, scissors, glue and a string. Fold the paper in half. Cut lines from the fold, but not to the end. Open it and make a circle. Glue the sides and add a handle. [Show the lantern]

Number two: a marker. You need a black marker to write your wish. Write it in big letters. You can write in English or in Spanish.

Number three: a wish. Think about one good wish. It can be for you, for your family, or for the world.

Number four: warm clothes. Pingxi is in the mountains. In February, it can be cold and rainy. People wear jackets and bring umbrellas.

Number five: a safe place. In Taiwan, people release lanterns in special areas. At our stand, we do not light anything. We hang our lanterns, and we use little electric candles.

Please remember: real sky lanterns can be dangerous. Always follow the rules, and think about the environment.

Thank you! Now my partner has some questions for you.

Traducción al español

¡Hola! Mi nombre es ___. Les diré cuándo es el Festival de los Faroles y qué se necesita.

Primero, cuándo. El Festival de los Faroles es cada año. Es el día quince del Año Nuevo Lunar, en febrero o marzo. Es la primera luna llena del año. En Pingxi, la gente también suelta faroles en otros días del año, pero el Festival de los Faroles es el día más especial. [Señala el calendario]

Ahora, ¿qué se necesita? Déjenme mostrarles. [Muestra cada objeto]

Número uno: un farol de papel. Necesitas papel de colores, tijeras, pegante y un cordel. Dobla el papel por la mitad. Corta líneas desde el doblez, pero no hasta el final. Ábrelo y forma un círculo. Pega los lados y agrega un asa. [Muestra el farol]

Número dos: un marcador. Necesitas un marcador negro para escribir tu deseo. Escríbelo en letras grandes. Puedes escribir en inglés o en español.

Número tres: un deseo. Piensa en un buen deseo. Puede ser para ti, para tu familia o para el mundo.

Número cuatro: ropa abrigada. Pingxi está en las montañas. En febrero puede hacer frío y llover. La gente usa chaquetas y lleva paraguas.

Número cinco: un lugar seguro. En Taiwán, la gente suelta los faroles en zonas especiales. En nuestro stand no encendemos nada. Colgamos nuestros faroles y usamos velitas eléctricas.

Por favor recuerden: los faroles voladores de verdad pueden ser peligrosos. Siempre sigan las reglas y piensen en el medio ambiente.

¡Gracias! Ahora mi compañero tiene algunas preguntas para ustedes.

Student 4 · Keyword, vocabulary and questions

Hello! My name is ___. Now it is your turn to participate. First, the keyword for your Festival Passport. The keyword for our festival is WISH. Please write it: W-I-S-H. Also write: Festival, Pingxi Lantern Festival. Country, Taiwan.

Now let's learn five new words. Please repeat after me. [Show each picture] Lantern. Sky. Wish. Hope. Safe. Choose one word for your passport. Write it in the box "one new word".

Let's play a game. I ask a question, and you answer.

Question one: In which country is Pingxi? Answer: Taiwan.

Question two: Why did people use lanterns long ago? Answer: To tell their families they were safe.

Question three: What do people write on the lantern? Answer: Their wishes.

Question four: Where does the lantern go? Answer: To the sky.

Question five: What do people eat at the Lantern Festival? Answer: Sweet rice balls, called tangyuan.

Question six: What does a lantern mean today? Answer: Hope and peace.

Very good! Now a riddle, like in the festival. Listen: "It has hands, but it cannot clap. What is it?" Think for ten seconds. Answer: A clock!

Now it is time to make a wish. [Give each person a small paper lantern and a marker] Write one wish in English. For example: "I want to learn English" or "I want my family to be happy". Hang your lantern on our wall.

Finally, write in your passport one thing people do at the festival. For example: "People write wishes" or "People send lanterns to the sky".

Let's say goodbye. Everybody together: Make a wish! Thank you for visiting our stand. Enjoy the next festival!

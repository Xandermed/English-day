🎨 Holi · India

4 presentadores · unos 5 minutos cada uno, todo en inglés. Lo que está entre corchetes es una acción (mostrar un objeto). La traducción es solo para estudiar.

Student 1 · Origin

Hello everyone! Welcome to our stand. My name is ___. Today we are going to talk about Holi. Holi is a very colorful festival from India. [Point to India on the map]

India is a big country in South Asia. Holi is a Hindu festival. It is many, many centuries old.

Now I want to tell you a story. Long ago, there was a bad king. He wanted everyone to worship him. But his son, Prahlad, loved the god Vishnu. The king was very angry. He asked his sister, Holika, to help him. Holika had a magic cloth. The cloth protected her from fire. She sat in a big fire with Prahlad. But the magic cloth flew to Prahlad and saved him. Holika burned in the fire. [Show the picture] Good won against evil!

Because of this story, on the night before Holi, people make a big bonfire. It is called Holika Dahan. The fire is a symbol that good wins over evil.

There is also a second story about the god Krishna. Krishna had blue skin. He was worried that a girl called Radha did not like it. His mother said, "Put color on Radha's face!" They played with colors and became friends. This is why people play with colors at Holi.

Now I will pass to my partner. Thank you!

Student 2 · How and why we celebrate

Hello! My name is ___. I am going to tell you how people celebrate Holi.

Holi has two days. On the first night, families and neighbors go to a big bonfire. They walk around the fire and sing songs. They say goodbye to the bad things of the year.

The next morning is the colorful day. People put on old white clothes. White is the best color, because the colors show very well. [Show a white T-shirt] Then they go outside and throw colored powder. The powder is called gulal. There are many colors: red, yellow, green, blue and pink. They also use water guns and water balloons. [Show the picture of the powder] People say "Happy Holi!" and put a little color on your face. Everybody becomes colorful. You cannot tell who is rich or poor, old or young. Everybody is equal.

During the day, people dance to loud music and play drums. They eat special sweets, like gujiya. Gujiya is a sweet dumpling. They also drink a cold drink called thandai. [Show the picture of the sweets]

Why do people celebrate? They celebrate because good wins over evil. They celebrate because spring is coming, and the fields are green and full of flowers. And Holi is a day to forgive. People forget old arguments, say sorry and make friends again.

Thank you! Now my partner will tell you when it is and what you need.

Traducción al español

¡Hola! Mi nombre es ___. Les voy a contar cómo celebra la gente Holi.

Holi dura dos días. La primera noche, las familias y los vecinos van a una gran hoguera. Caminan alrededor del fuego y cantan canciones. Se despiden de las cosas malas del año.

La mañana siguiente es el día colorido. La gente se pone ropa blanca vieja. El blanco es el mejor color, porque los colores se ven muy bien. [Muestra una camiseta blanca] Luego salen y se lanzan polvos de colores. El polvo se llama gulal. Hay muchos colores: rojo, amarillo, verde, azul y rosado. También usan pistolas de agua y globos de agua. [Muestra la imagen del polvo] La gente dice "¡Feliz Holi!" y te pone un poco de color en la cara. Todos se vuelven coloridos. No se sabe quién es rico o pobre, viejo o joven. Todos son iguales.

Durante el día, la gente baila con música fuerte y toca tambores. Comen dulces especiales, como el gujiya. El gujiya es una empanadita dulce. También toman una bebida fría llamada thandai. [Muestra la imagen de los dulces]

¿Por qué celebra la gente? Celebran porque el bien vence al mal. Celebran porque llega la primavera y los campos están verdes y llenos de flores. Y Holi es un día para perdonar. La gente olvida las peleas viejas, pide perdón y vuelve a ser amiga.

Student 3 · When and what we need

Hello! My name is ___. I will tell you when Holi is and what you need.

First, when. Holi is every year. It is on the full moon in March, or sometimes at the end of February. It is the beginning of spring. In India, winter ends and the weather is warm. The most famous places for Holi are Mathura and Vrindavan. Thousands of people go there. [Point to the map]

Now, what do you need? Let me show you. [Show each item]

Number one: a white T-shirt. Old clothes are best, because they get dirty.

Number two: colors. In India, people use colored powder. At our stand, we use colored paper confetti, because it is clean and safe. You can cut small pieces of red, yellow, green, blue and pink paper. You can also use real flower petals.

Number three: a big bowl for the colors. Put the colors in a bowl and share them with your friends. [Show the bowl]

Number four: a paper color splash for decoration. It is easy. Cut a circle with a wavy edge. Write "Happy Holi" on it. Hang it on the wall.

Number five: sweets and music. Bring a sweet to share and a happy song.

Please remember: in a real Holi, people protect their eyes and skin. Use only safe colors. Never throw colors at a person who says no.

Thank you! Now my partner has some questions for you.

Traducción al español

Número tres: un tazón grande para los colores. Pon los colores en un tazón y compártelos con tus amigos. [Muestra el tazón]

Student 4 · Keyword, vocabulary and questions

Hello! My name is ___. Now it is your turn to participate. First, the keyword for your Festival Passport. The keyword for our festival is COLORS. Please write it: C-O-L-O-R-S. Also write: Festival, Holi. Country, India.

Now let's learn five new words. Please repeat after me. [Show each picture] Powder. Spring. Bonfire. Sweet. Forgive. Choose one word for your passport. Write it in the box "one new word".

Let's play a game. I ask a question, and you answer.

Question one: In which country is Holi? Answer: India.

Question two: What do people throw at Holi? Answer: Colored powder.

Question three: What is the name of the big fire the night before? Answer: Holika Dahan.

Question four: What season is it? Answer: Spring.

Question five: What color of clothes do people wear? Answer: White.

Question six: What do people do with old arguments? Answer: They forgive and forget.

Very good! Now a challenge. Let's make a rainbow. [Give each person a piece of colored paper] Each person says a color in English and puts the paper on the board. Can we make seven colors in one minute?

Finally, write in your passport one thing people do at Holi. For example: "People throw colored powder" or "People eat sweets".

Let's say goodbye. Everybody together: Happy Holi! Thank you for visiting our stand. Enjoy the next festival!

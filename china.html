4 presentadores · unos 5 minutos cada uno, todo en inglés. Lo que está entre corchetes es una acción (mostrar un objeto). La traducción es solo para estudiar.

Student 1 · Origin

Hello everyone! Welcome to our stand. My name is ___. Today we are going to talk about Chinese New Year. It is the most important festival in China. [Point to China on the map]

China is a big country in Asia. Chinese New Year is also called Spring Festival. It is more than 3,000 years old.

Now, I want to tell you an old story. Long ago, there was a monster called Nian. Nian was big and very scary. It lived in the mountains. Every year, at the end of winter, Nian came to the villages. It ate the food and it scared the people.

One day, a wise old man came to a village. He saw that the monster was afraid of three things: the color red, loud noise and light. So the villagers put red paper on their doors. They made a lot of noise with drums. They lit many lights. Nian was very scared and ran away! [Show something red]

Since that day, people in China use red decorations and noise at the New Year. They say this keeps the monster away and brings good luck.

Chinese New Year follows the lunar calendar. The lunar calendar follows the moon, not the sun. For this reason, the date is different every year. It is in January or February.

Every year also has an animal. There are twelve animals: rat, ox, tiger, rabbit, dragon, snake, horse, goat, monkey, rooster, dog and pig. [Show the animal poster] Which animal are you? Think about the year you were born.

Now I will pass to my partner. He or she will tell you how people celebrate. Thank you!

Student 2 · How and why we celebrate

Hello! My name is ___. I am going to tell you how people celebrate Chinese New Year. The celebration lasts fifteen days.

First, before the New Year, families clean the house. They clean to sweep away bad luck. But after the New Year starts, they do not clean for a few days, because they do not want to sweep away the good luck!

Then, families decorate. They put red paper with good wishes on the doors. They hang red lanterns. [Show a red lantern]

On New Year's Eve, the whole family eats a big dinner together. It is called the reunion dinner. Families eat dumplings, fish and noodles. Dumplings look like old Chinese money, so they bring wealth. Fish is important because in Chinese the word for fish sounds like the word for "extra", so it means more money every year. Noodles are very long, so they mean a long life. [Show a picture of the food]

At midnight, people make a lot of noise with fireworks to say goodbye to the old year.

The next day, children say "Happy New Year" to their parents and grandparents. Then they get a red envelope. Inside there is some money. The red envelope brings good luck to the child. [Show a red envelope]

People also watch the lion dance and the dragon dance in the streets. The dancers move to loud drums. It is very colorful and exciting.

Why do people celebrate? They celebrate to say goodbye to bad luck, to welcome a new start, and to be with their family. Family is the most important thing in this festival.

Thank you! Now my partner will tell you when it is and what you need.

Traducción al español

Primero, antes del Año Nuevo, las familias limpian la casa. Limpian para barrer la mala suerte. Pero cuando empieza el Año Nuevo, no limpian durante unos días, ¡porque no quieren barrer la buena suerte!

Luego las familias decoran. Ponen papel rojo con buenos deseos en las puertas. Cuelgan faroles rojos. [Muestra un farol rojo]

En la Nochevieja, toda la familia come una gran cena juntos. Se llama la cena de reunión. Las familias comen dumplings, pescado y fideos. Los dumplings parecen el dinero antiguo chino, así que traen riqueza. El pescado es importante porque en chino la palabra "pescado" suena como la palabra "extra", y significa más dinero cada año. Los fideos son muy largos, así que significan una vida larga. [Muestra una imagen de la comida]

A medianoche, la gente hace mucho ruido con fuegos artificiales para despedir el año viejo.

Al día siguiente, los niños dicen "Feliz Año Nuevo" a sus padres y abuelos. Luego reciben un sobre rojo. Adentro hay algo de dinero. El sobre rojo trae buena suerte al niño. [Muestra un sobre rojo]

La gente también ve la danza del león y la danza del dragón en las calles. Los bailarines se mueven al ritmo de tambores fuertes. Es muy colorido y emocionante.

¿Por qué celebra la gente? Celebran para despedir la mala suerte, dar la bienvenida a un nuevo comienzo y estar con su familia. La familia es lo más importante de este festival.

Student 3 · When and what we need

Hello! My name is ___. I am going to tell you when Chinese New Year is and what you need to celebrate it.

First, when. Chinese New Year is every year. The date changes because it follows the moon. It is between January 21 and February 20. The celebration lasts fifteen days. The last day is called the Lantern Festival. On that day, people hang many lanterns and look at the full moon.

Now, what do you need? Let me show you. [Show each item]

Number one: red clothes. In China, red is a lucky color. People wear new red clothes for the New Year.

Number two: red paper. You can cut red paper and write a good wish on it. For example, "Happy New Year" or "Good luck". In Chinese we say "Xin Nian Kuai Le". It means "Happy New Year". Another famous phrase is "Gong Xi Fa Cai". It means "Wishing you wealth". Please repeat with me: Gong Xi Fa Cai!

Number three: a red envelope. You can make one with red paper. Inside you can put a small message or a sweet.

Number four: a paper lantern. You need red paper, scissors and glue. It is easy. First, fold the paper in half. Second, cut lines from the fold, but not to the end. Third, open the paper and make a circle. Fourth, glue the two sides. Finally, add a handle. [Show the lantern]

Number five: food. People eat oranges, because they are a symbol of luck, and sweet snacks.

At our stand, we brought red paper, lanterns and red envelopes. You can look at them and take photos. Thank you! Now my partner has some questions for you.

Traducción al español

¡Hola! Mi nombre es ___. Les voy a decir cuándo es el Año Nuevo Chino y qué se necesita para celebrarlo.

Primero, cuándo. El Año Nuevo Chino es cada año. La fecha cambia porque sigue la luna. Es entre el 21 de enero y el 20 de febrero. La celebración dura quince días. El último día se llama el Festival de los Faroles. Ese día la gente cuelga muchos faroles y mira la luna llena.

Ahora, ¿qué se necesita? Déjenme mostrarles. [Muestra cada objeto]

Número uno: ropa roja. En China el rojo es un color de suerte. La gente usa ropa roja nueva para el Año Nuevo.

Número dos: papel rojo. Puedes cortar papel rojo y escribir un buen deseo. Por ejemplo, "Feliz Año Nuevo" o "Buena suerte". En chino decimos "Xin Nian Kuai Le". Significa "Feliz Año Nuevo". Otra frase famosa es "Gong Xi Fa Cai". Significa "Te deseo riqueza". Repitan conmigo: ¡Gong Xi Fa Cai!

Número tres: un sobre rojo. Puedes hacer uno con papel rojo. Adentro puedes poner un mensaje pequeño o un dulce.

Número cuatro: un farol de papel. Necesitas papel rojo, tijeras y pegante. Es fácil. Primero, dobla el papel por la mitad. Segundo, corta líneas desde el doblez, pero no hasta el final. Tercero, abre el papel y forma un círculo. Cuarto, pega los dos lados. Finalmente, agrega un asa. [Muestra el farol]

Número cinco: comida. La gente come naranjas, porque son un símbolo de suerte, y dulces.

En nuestro stand traemos papel rojo, faroles y sobres rojos. Pueden mirarlos y tomar fotos. ¡Gracias! Ahora mi compañero tiene algunas preguntas para ustedes.

Student 4 · Keyword, vocabulary and questions

Hello! My name is ___. Now it is your turn to participate. First, I will give you the keyword for your Festival Passport. The keyword for our festival is RED. Please write it: R-E-D. Also write: Festival, Chinese New Year. Country, China.

Now let's learn five new words. Please repeat after me. [Show each picture] Lantern. Dumpling. Envelope. Lucky. Dragon. Choose one word for your passport. Write it in the box "one new word".

Let's play a quick game. I say a question, and you answer. Are you ready?

Question one: What is the name of the monster? Answer: Nian.

Question two: What color is lucky in China? Answer: Red.

Question three: What is inside a red envelope? Answer: Money.

Question four: How many days does the celebration last? Answer: Fifteen days.

Question five: What does the fish mean at dinner? Answer: Extra money every year.

Question six: What is the last day called? Answer: The Lantern Festival.

Very good! Now, a challenge. Point to something red in this room. Who can find three red things in one minute? [Give a small sticker to the winner]

Finally, write in your passport one thing people do at Chinese New Year. For example: "People eat dumplings" or "People get red envelopes".

Let's say goodbye the Chinese way. Everybody together: Gong Xi Fa Cai! Thank you for visiting our stand. Enjoy the next festival!
